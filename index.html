Phishing Awareness Simulator

Can you spot a phishing attempt?

Example Email

From: Amazon Support <support-amazon@shady-link.com>

Subject: Urgent! Your account has been compromised!

Click the link below to secure your account immediately:

http://amazon-security-check-now.com/login

How to Spot Phishing:

  • Check the sender's email address carefully.
  • Look for misspellings or urgent scare tactics.
  • Never click on suspicious links — go directly to the official site.
